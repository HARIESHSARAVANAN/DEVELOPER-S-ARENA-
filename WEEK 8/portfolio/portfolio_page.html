03 • KEY INSIGHTS

THE SIGNALS
BEHIND CHURN

EARLY-TENURE CUSTOMERS

Customers who churn show substantially shorter tenure in the analyzed sample. This makes early lifecycle engagement an important retention segment.

CONTRACT TYPE

Contract structure provides a useful segmentation dimension because observed churn rates differ across contract groups.

MONTHLY CHARGES

Monthly charges differ between churned and retained customers, suggesting pricing and perceived service value deserve further investigation.

PREDICTIVE PRIORITIZATION

A churn model can help prioritize customers for human review, but predictions should not be treated as causal explanations.

07 • TRANSPARENCY

DATA LIMITATIONS

The supplied dataset contains 500 customer records. It does not include revenue, profit, customer acquisition cost, marketing spend or intervention-history fields. Therefore, financial ROI and causal intervention effects cannot be calculated from this dataset alone.


The predictive model should be validated against newer operational data before being used in a production retention workflow.
